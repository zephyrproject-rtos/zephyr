CRA Horizontal Standards (EN 40000-1)
#####################################

.. warning::
   This document is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice.
   Consult with your legal counsel for compliance guidance specific to your situation.

.. note::
   The EN 40000-1 series is still under development. The information on EN 40000-1-4 is based on
   the draft presented by CEN/CLC/JTC 13/WG 9 in the `EN 40000-1-4 deep dive session`_ of
   March 5, 2026. Requirement IDs, titles and scope are subject to change until the standard is
   published.

Overview
********

The European Commission requested harmonized standards for the
:ref:`Cyber Resilience Act <cra_faq>` (CRA) in `Standardisation Request M/606`_. They fall into
two groups:

* **Horizontal standards** apply to all products with digital elements. CEN and CENELEC develop
  them in the joint technical committee CEN/CLC/JTC 13 as the EN 40000-1 series.
* **Vertical standards** add requirements for the important and critical product categories of
  the CRA. Most of them are developed by ETSI as the EN 304 6xx series, including standards for
  operating systems (EN 304 626) and boot managers (EN 304 623).

A product that does not fall into one of the categories covered by a vertical standard relies on
the horizontal standards alone. All standards are expected to be published before the CRA
applies in full on December 11, 2027.

Parts of the Series
*******************

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Part
     - Title
     - Scope
   * - EN 40000-1-1
     - Vocabulary
     - Terms and definitions used across the series.
   * - EN 40000-1-2
     - Principles for cyber resilience
     - Process standard for the risk-based design, development and maintenance of products,
       covering CRA Annex I Part I (1). The risk assessment it defines selects the security
       controls from EN 40000-1-4.
   * - EN 40000-1-3
     - Vulnerability handling
     - Process standard for vulnerability handling, covering CRA Annex I Part II.
   * - EN 40000-1-4
     - Generic security requirements
     - Product standard with a catalog of security controls, covering CRA Annex I Part I (2),
       points (a) to (m).
   * - TR 40000-1-5
     - Threats and security objectives
     - Informative technical report that maps threats to security objectives, as an input to the
       risk assessment.

EN 40000-1-1 to EN 40000-1-3 are available as drafts. EN 40000-1-4 is at an earlier stage and no
public draft is available yet.

For Zephyr, EN 40000-1-2 and EN 40000-1-3 mainly concern the processes of the manufacturer and of
the Zephyr Project, which are described in :ref:`cra_faq`. The rest of this page covers
EN 40000-1-4, which defines requirements on the product itself.

Generic Security Requirements (EN 40000-1-4)
********************************************

Structure
=========

EN 40000-1-4 builds on the :ref:`EN 18031 <en_18031>` series. It keeps the EN 18031 security
mechanisms and requirement IDs and adds security controls for the CRA essential requirements that
EN 18031 does not cover. The standard is structured as follows:

* Each CRA essential requirement of Annex I Part I (2) is mapped to security objectives.
* Each security objective is met by one or more security controls, which can have several levels.
* Each security control has assessment criteria.

Unlike EN 18031, EN 40000-1-4 does not decide whether a control applies through decision trees.
Which controls are needed, and at which level, follows from the risk assessment according to
EN 40000-1-2. Informative annexes map the security objectives to other standards such as
IEC 62443-4-2, ISO/IEC 27002 and :ref:`ETSI EN 303 645 <etsi_303645>`.

A product that already meets EN 18031 has covered a large part of the CRA product requirements.
The remaining gaps are addressed by the new requirements below.
