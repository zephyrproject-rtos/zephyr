RED Cybersecurity Standards (EN 18031)
######################################

.. warning::
   This document is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice.
   Consult with your legal counsel for compliance guidance specific to your situation.

Overview
********

The Radio Equipment Directive (RED, `Directive 2014/53/EU`_) defines essential requirements for
radio equipment placed on the EU market. Its cybersecurity requirements in Article 3(3), points
(d), (e) and (f), were activated by `Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30`_ and have been mandatory
for new radio equipment since August 1, 2025.

EN 18031 is the series of harmonized standards developed by CEN-CENELEC for these requirements.
Applying it is voluntary, but equipment that meets the applicable parts benefits from a
presumption of conformity with the corresponding essential requirements, within the restrictions
listed when the standards were cited in the Official Journal of the European Union by
`Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/138`_.

.. admonition:: Key Dates
   :class: important

   * **January 12, 2022**: Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 published
   * **August 2024**: EN 18031-1, EN 18031-2 and EN 18031-3 published by CEN-CENELEC
   * **January 30, 2025**: EN 18031 cited in the Official Journal, with restrictions
   * **August 1, 2025**: Cybersecurity requirements of the RED apply to new radio equipment
   * **December 11, 2027**: The Cyber Resilience Act applies in full

The Cyber Resilience Act (:ref:`cra_faq`) covers the same products with broader requirements. The
Commission has announced that the Delegated Regulation will be repealed once the CRA applies, so
EN 18031 is mainly relevant for radio equipment placed on the market before December 11, 2027.
The EN 18031 mechanisms map to a large part of the CRA essential requirements, so work done for
EN 18031 carries over.

EN 18031 applies to radio equipment as a whole, not to its software components. Zephyr cannot be
certified against EN 18031 on its own, and the Zephyr Project has no obligations under the RED.
This page explains the structure of the standard and lists the Zephyr features that manufacturers
can use to meet its requirements.

Structure of the Standard
*************************

Parts of the standard
=====================

EN 18031 consists of three parts, one for each of the RED cybersecurity requirements. A product
has to meet every part that applies to it.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 15 15 35 35

   * - Part
     - RED article
     - Objective
     - Equipment in scope
   * - EN 18031-1
     - 3(3)(d)
     - Radio equipment does not harm the network or its functioning, and does not misuse network
       resources.
     - Internet-connected radio equipment.
   * - EN 18031-2
     - 3(3)(e)
     - Radio equipment protects the personal data and privacy of the user and subscriber.
     - Internet-connected radio equipment that can process personal data, radio equipment
       intended for childcare, toys and wearable radio equipment.
   * - EN 18031-3
     - 3(3)(f)
     - Radio equipment protects against fraud.
     - Internet-connected radio equipment that enables the transfer of money, monetary value or
       virtual currency.

Internet-connected radio equipment is equipment that can communicate over the internet itself,
either directly or through other equipment such as a gateway, a router or a smartphone.

Assets
======

Each part of EN 18031 protects a set of assets. The requirements refer to these assets to define
what an access control, secure storage or secure communication mechanism has to protect.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 60

   * - Asset
     - Parts
     - Examples
   * - Security assets
     - 1, 2, 3
     - Cryptographic keys, passwords and other authenticators, security configuration, and the
       security functions of the equipment.
   * - Network assets
     - 1
     - Network functions and their configuration, such as routing and interface settings.
   * - Privacy assets
     - 2
     - Personal information and the functions that process it, such as a camera, a microphone or
       location data.
   * - Financial assets
     - 3
     - Financial data and the functions that process transactions, such as payment credentials.

Decision trees and assessment
=============================

EN 18031 does not prescribe specific technologies. Instead, each requirement comes with a
rationale, guidance, examples and assessment criteria. The assessment is based on a decision tree
for every requirement: the manufacturer answers its questions for each relevant asset, interface or
mechanism of the equipment, and the path through the tree results in one of the following
verdicts:

* **PASS**: the requirement is met.
* **FAIL**: the requirement is not met.
* **NOT APPLICABLE**: the requirement does not apply, for example because the equipment has no
  asset or interface of the relevant kind.

The assessment criteria of each requirement distinguish between the following kinds of
assessment:

* **Conceptual assessment**: the documented design follows the decision tree to a PASS or NOT
  APPLICABLE verdict.
* **Functional completeness assessment**: the documentation covers all relevant assets,
  interfaces and mechanisms of the equipment.
* **Functional sufficiency assessment**: the implemented mechanisms work as documented, which
  may require testing the equipment.

The results of earlier requirements are inputs to later ones. For example, the assets identified
for ACM-1 determine which authentication mechanisms are needed for AUM-1.

For Manufacturers Using Zephyr
******************************

Does EN 18031 apply to my product?
==================================

EN 18031 is relevant if your product is radio equipment under the RED, which is the case for any
product that intentionally emits or receives radio waves, for example over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi,
IEEE 802.15.4, LoRa or cellular. It applies to products placed on the EU market on or after
August 1, 2025, independent of when the product was first designed.

Products that only use a wired connection are not in scope of the RED, but are in scope of the
CRA. Some product categories that are covered by their own Union legislation, such as medical
devices, in vitro diagnostic medical devices, motor vehicles, civil aviation products and
electronic road toll systems, are excluded from some or all of the RED cybersecurity requirements.
See Article 1 of `Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30`_ for the exact exclusions.

Which parts of EN 18031 apply to my product?
============================================

Answer the following questions for your product:

#. Can the product communicate over the internet, directly or through a gateway, a router or a
   smartphone? If yes, EN 18031-1 applies.
#. Can the product process personal data, or is it intended for childcare, a toy, or wearable?
   If yes, EN 18031-2 applies. Childcare equipment, toys and wearables are in scope even without
   an internet connection.
#. Can the product transfer money, monetary value or virtual currency? If yes, EN 18031-3
   applies.

Most internet-connected products fall under EN 18031-1 and EN 18031-2, since user accounts,
identifiers and location data already count as personal data.

.. _en_18031_restrictions:

Can I rely on EN 18031 for presumption of conformity?
=====================================================

Yes, within the restrictions listed in `Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/138`_. If one of the
following restrictions applies to your product, meeting the affected requirement does not give a
presumption of conformity:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 15 65

   * - Requirements
     - Parts
     - Restriction
   * - AUM-5-1, AUM-5-2
     - 1, 2, 3
     - The equipment lets the user choose not to use any password.
   * - ACM-3 to ACM-6
     - 2
     - The access control mechanism categories used are not compatible with parental or guardian
       control.
   * - SUM-2
     - 3
     - The equipment processes financial assets and relies on only one of the update verification
       methods listed in the standard. None of them is sufficient on its own.

If you apply EN 18031 in full and none of the restrictions apply to your product, you can use
internal production control (Module A, RED Annex II) and declare conformity yourself. Otherwise,
for example if you apply the standard only in part or rely on an excluded option, you have to
involve a notified body through EU-type examination (Module B and C, RED Annex III) or full
quality assurance (Module H, RED Annex IV).

What documentation do I need to provide?
========================================

The technical documentation required by RED Annex V has to show how the equipment meets the
applicable parts of EN 18031. For each requirement, it typically includes:

* The assets, interfaces and mechanisms of the equipment that the requirement applies to.
* The path through the decision tree and the resulting verdict, with a justification for each
  answer.
* A description of how the mechanisms are implemented, for example which Zephyr subsystems and
  Kconfig options are used.

Some requirements, such as GEC-4 and GEC-7, ask for documentation of the equipment's interfaces
and capabilities themselves. The EU declaration of conformity lists the parts of EN 18031 that
were applied.

Zephyr provides information that can be used in this documentation:

* The build configuration (``zephyr/.config``) and the devicetree (``zephyr/zephyr.dts``) in the
  build directory document which subsystems, services and interfaces are included in the image.
* :ref:`west-spdx` generates a software bill of materials for the image.
* :ref:`Zephyr Vulnerabilities <vulnerabilities>` lists known vulnerabilities and the releases
  that fix them.

Mapping to Zephyr Features
**************************

The following tables list the requirements of EN 18031 and the Zephyr features that can support a
product in meeting them. The parts of the standard that use each mechanism are listed in
:ref:`en_18031_mechanisms`. The mapping is informative: whether a requirement applies and whether
it is met is decided by the manufacturer for the complete product, following the decision trees in
the standard. The requirement text is only available in the standard itself.

Requirements marked as product responsibility are not addressed by Zephyr and have to be
implemented by the application or the product's hardware and processes.
