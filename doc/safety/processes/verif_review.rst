Process
*******

Purpose & Scope
===============

The verification review process aims at defining when and how shall contributors perform review of safety artefacts.

This process is based on the established :ref:`General Guidelines <contribute_to_zephyr>` for contribution.

This process is applicable to Zephyr project safety releases and applies to all of the software life cycle data, software tools and hardware tools produced and used throughout the development life cycle.
