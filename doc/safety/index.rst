.. _safety_section:

Safety
######


Zephyr provides a comprehensive set of documents for integrators to use the RTOS in safety critical applications.
These documents describe the Zephyr processes, developer and integrators guidelines and requirements with regards to safety.
Ultimately, it is the integrators' responsibility to ensure that compliance to their target safety standards is reached within their project.
