Everest ES8311
##############

See the `ES8311 datasheet`_ for electrical specifications and the `ES8311 user guide`_
for register descriptions and configuration details.

The ES8311 driver supports mono playback from the left channel of a two-channel I2S stream.
The codec consumes externally generated MCLK, bit clock and frame clock. Supported word
sizes are 16, 18, 20, 24 and 32 bits, with sample rates from 8 to 48 kHz. The supplied MCLK
must be convertible to 256 times the sample rate using a divider from 1 to 8 and a
multiplier of 1, 2, 4 or 8. The driver keeps the pre-divider output below 4.5 MHz.

Enable :kconfig:option:`CONFIG_AUDIO` and :kconfig:option:`CONFIG_AUDIO_CODEC` and describe
the codec with the :dtcompatible:`everest,es8311` binding. Set ``mclk_freq`` in
:c:struct:`audio_codec_cfg` to the actual MCLK frequency and select clock target mode for
both clocks in the I2S configuration.

``AUDIO_PROPERTY_OUTPUT_VOLUME`` uses whole decibels from -95 to +32, with a default of
0 dB. Volume and mute accept ``AUDIO_CHANNEL_FRONT_LEFT`` or ``AUDIO_CHANNEL_ALL``.
Call :c:func:`audio_codec_apply_properties` after changing these properties.

An optional ``vin-supply`` controls codec power. An optional ``pa-gpios`` enables an
external amplifier during playback. With :kconfig:option:`CONFIG_PM_DEVICE`, suspend
powers down the codec and releases its supply; resume restores the configuration and
properties. Stop playback before requesting suspend.

Microphone capture and codec-generated clocks are not supported.
