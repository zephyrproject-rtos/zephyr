.. zephyr:board:: esp32s31_devkitc

Overview
********

ESP32-S31-DevKitC is a development board based on the ESP32-S31, a dual-core
RISC-V SoC with dual-band Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth LE, and IEEE 802.15.4
connectivity, working with external SPI flash and PSRAM.

Hardware
********

Most of the I/O pins are broken out to the pin headers on both sides for easy
interfacing. Developers can either connect peripherals with jumper wires or
mount ESP32-S31-DevKitC on a breadboard.
