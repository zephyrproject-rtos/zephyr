.. zephyr:board:: esp32s3_touch_lcd_2_8c

Overview
********

The `ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8C`_ is an ESP32-S3 development board from Waveshare with a 2.8-inch round
LCD, touch screen, IMU, RTC, microSD card slot and Li-Po battery charger.
