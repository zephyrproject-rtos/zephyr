.. zephyr:board:: esp32s3_touch_lcd_2_8

Overview
********

The ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8 is a development board equipped with the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1
module and a 2.8" LCD touch screen. This board integrates complete Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
Low Energy functions. For more information, check `ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8`_.
